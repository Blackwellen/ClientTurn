# Orbas CRM

> Orbas CRM is a multi-app business operating suite from Blackwellen Limited, spanning CRM, accounting, operations, projects, people/HR, service, documents, analytics, compliance, charity and automations under one workspace, with a white-label client portal.

Built by Blackwellen Limited (company number 16482166, England and Wales) — the same company behind [ClientTurn](/llms.txt).

Status: in active development. The current build is broad and architecturally complete, but is still moving from UI scaffolding with representative sample data to fully wired live data — do not treat feature areas as independently verified in production yet.

## Modules

CRM, Accounting, Operations, Projects, People/HR, Service, Documents, Analytics, Compliance, Charity, Automations, and Connect, plus an onboarding wizard, an app-launcher workspace switcher and a platform-admin console.
