# Caption Fox

> Caption Fox is a social-media management platform for brands, creators and agencies that combines AI content generation with campaign management — "run campaigns, not just posts."

Built by Blackwellen Limited (company number 16482166, England and Wales) — the same company behind [ClientTurn](/llms.txt).

## What it does (per its marketing site)

- AI-generated captions, scripts, hooks and hashtags.
- Drag-and-drop content calendar.
- Twelve campaign types, including giveaways, contests and UGC programmes.
- A user-generated-content workflow.
- A unified social inbox with AI-drafted replies.
- Multi-platform analytics across Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest and YouTube.

## Who it's for

Growing brands, content creators and marketing agencies managing multiple social channels.

## Pricing (per its marketing site)

- Free — 1 brand, 3 posts/month.
- Pro — £59/month, 5 brands, unlimited posts.
- Agency — £199/month, unlimited brands, white-label.
