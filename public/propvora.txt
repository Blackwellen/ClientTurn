# Propvora

> Propvora is a UK property-operations platform for landlords, portfolio operators and letting agencies — one workspace for portfolio, money, work, compliance, planning, legal, accounting, contacts, calendar and messaging.

Built by Blackwellen Limited (company number 16482166, England and Wales) — the same company behind [ClientTurn](/llms.txt). Live marketing site: propvora.com.

Status: feature-complete for a UK V1 as of the current build, but not yet approved for general availability — treat as pre-launch.

## What it does

- Compliance deadline tracking: Gas Safety, EICR, EPC, HMO licences, Legionella, Fire safety, PAT, and Renters' Rights Act 2026 requirements.
- Portfolio management: properties, units, tenancies, contacts and documents in one record set.
- Work and maintenance scheduling on a unified calendar.
- Money and accounting: income, expenses, invoices and arrears.
- An AI Copilot that assists but is human-reviewed before any action executes.
- Separate tenant, landlord and supplier portals.
- Multi-user teams with Stripe billing and row-level-security-isolated multi-tenant data.

## Who it's for

UK landlords and portfolio operators, from individual landlords through growing letting-agency teams.
