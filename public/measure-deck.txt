# Measure Deck

> Measure Deck is a commercial-management platform from Blackwellen Limited for UK construction quantity surveyors and commercial teams, covering change events, payment applications, cost/value reconciliation, drawings and final accounts.

Built by Blackwellen Limited (company number 16482166, England and Wales) — the same company behind [ClientTurn](/llms.txt).

Status: in active development and not yet released to production customers. Treat it as pre-release rather than a shipped, generally-available product.

## What it does

- Change-event tracking and payment applications.
- Cost/value reconciliation (CVR).
- Evidence, drawings and BIM handling.
- Final accounts and commercial reporting.
- Supplier and contact management, tasks and scheduling.
- An AI copilot for commercial-management workflows.

## Who it's for

UK specialist subcontractors and SME main contractors, and the quantity surveyors and commercial managers who run their contracts under JCT, NEC3 and NEC4 forms.
