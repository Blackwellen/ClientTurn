**Settings → Billing & Usage** is **owner-only**. Anyone else who opens it sees **Billing is owner-only** and is asked to speak to the owner.

## What is on the page

| Panel | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Current plan** | Your plan and what it includes, the billing period, the next billing date (or **Access ends** if the subscription is ending), and the monthly price. Buttons: **Upgrade to** the next plan, and **Manage billing (Stripe)** |
| **Subscription status** | Active, trialling, payment failed or cancelled, with what that means, the trial end date, and the card on file |
| **Usage this month** | Leads, messages and team members against your plan, and the date usage resets |
| **Recent invoices** | Your latest invoices with a **Download** link, and **View all invoices** in the Stripe portal |
| **Usage & limits** | Every limit, this period and today, with top-up credit and what happens at the limit |
| **Message credits** | Your SMS and WhatsApp credit balances, bundles to **Buy**, and **Purchase history** |
| **Monthly usage overview** | Prospects sourced, messages sent, intent monitors and search runs |
| **Communication allocation** | How your message allowance is split between channels, and **Daily sending limits (all channels)** |
| **Overage control** | **Allow automatic overage** and your **Monthly additional spend cap** |
| **Channel usage** and **Usage history** | Sent, delivery and replies per channel this month, and past months |
| **AI allowance** | AI tokens used and left, roughly how many assistant replies remain, **Buy more tokens**, and **Recent top-ups** |
| **Billing help** | Links to update your payment method, view invoices or contact support |

The Stripe portal buttons become available once billing has started.

## Common tasks

- **Update your card:** **Manage billing (Stripe)** or **Update payment method**. See [What happens if a payment fails](/help/billing/failed-payments).
- **Upgrade, downgrade or cancel:** see [Changing your plan](/help/billing/changing-your-plan).
- **Buy more SMS, WhatsApp or AI:** see [Top-up credits for AI, SMS and WhatsApp](/help/billing/top-up-credits).
- **Understand a limit or change a daily cap:** see [Usage and limits](/help/billing/usage-and-limits).
