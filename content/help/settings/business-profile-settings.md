**Settings → Business Profile** holds what ClientTurn believes about your business, who you want to reach, and what counts as success. The conversation assistant, Find Leads and your agents all work from it. Owners and admins can edit it.

## What we know about your business

Enter your **Website** and choose **Analyse**. ClientTurn reads your site to understand what you sell. It shows how many pages it read, when, and a short summary.

## Business facts

**Business facts** lists everything ClientTurn holds about your business and where each fact came from:

| Badge | Where the fact came from |
|---|---|
| You told us | Typed in by your team |
| From your website | Read from your site |
| From a connection | From a connected service |
| Learned from results | From your own results |
| Inferred | Worked out by ClientTurn. Marked **Worth checking** |

To add a fact yourself, enter a **Fact** and a **Value** and choose **Add**. Anything you type is treated as verified and locked.

For each fact you can:

- **Confirm this is right**, which marks a fact ClientTurn found as verified.
- **Lock this fact**, which stops it being changed automatically. **Allow automatic updates** unlocks it.

## How facts reach the assistant

Before it writes, the assistant is given a short **offer card**: a summary of what you sell and how you sound. Only trusted information goes on it:

- **Facts** only if a person confirmed or locked them, or your team typed them in. A fact read from your website or inferred by ClientTurn stays off the card until someone confirms it.
- Facts past their valid-until date are left out.
- Anything that looks like internal commercial data, such as costs, margins, price bands or deal values, is always left out.
- **Services** by name and description. A price reaches the card only as published price wording, never from a service's **Average value**.
- Your **Outreach guidance**: value proposition, key messages and proof points.

The card has a fixed size. If there is too much, whole items are dropped, lowest priority first, and never cut off part-way through a sentence.

> **Tip:** If the assistant is not mentioning something true and important, check whether it is a fact still marked **Worth checking**. Confirming it lets the assistant use it.

The assistant never quotes a price you have not published, and never promises a time or an area. See [Setting up AI agents](/help/ai-agents/ai-agents-setup).

## Outreach guidance

**Outreach guidance** sets how ClientTurn represents your business:

- **Tone:** how your messages should sound.
- **Value proposition:** the one sentence that explains why someone should care.
- **Key messages:** the points worth making.
- **Proof points:** facts you can stand behind if challenged.
- **Call to action:** what you want the reader to do next.
- **Avoid:** anything that should never appear in a message.
- **Claims we cannot make:** guarantees, prices or timescales you cannot promise. Generated copy will not use them.

Choose **Save guidance**.

## Ideal customer profiles

Describe who you sell to. Find Leads cannot score anyone without at least one profile. Choose **New profile** and fill in **Name**, **Description**, **Industries**, **Locations**, **Smallest company**, **Largest company** and **Decision makers** (the job titles worth reaching).

## Conversion goals

Say what success looks like, so every campaign and agent knows what to ask for. Choose **New goal**, then pick **What should happen?**: Book an appointment, Book a site visit, Book a demo, Request a quote, Get a phone call, Sign up, Buy, Hand over to a person, or Something else. Add **Where does it go?**, the `https://` address the lead is sent to.

## What we have learned

Patterns from your own results. A finding only appears once there is enough data for it to mean something.
