Write to Copilot the way you would to a colleague. Specific questions get specific answers. These examples all use things Copilot can actually do.

## Understand your pipeline

- "Which leads need attention?"
- "Summarise this lead and recommend the next action." (open the lead first)
- "Why is this lead graded C? What is missing from its score?"
- "Can I contact this lead on WhatsApp? If not, why not?"
- "Show me my qualified leads."
- "Are there any possible duplicate leads waiting for a decision?"

## Performance

- "Summarise my analytics for the last 30 days."
- "Show me the funnel from new lead to won for this month."
- "How are my campaigns performing?"
- "How much AI have we used this month against our limit?"

## Prospects and sourcing

- "Show prospects graded A that are waiting for review."
- "What evidence is behind this prospect's score?"
- "Approve this prospect for outreach." (only once its contactability is confirmed)

## Doing things

- "Assign this lead to Sam and add a note that they prefer email."
- "Mark this lead as qualified."
- "Draft a reply to this lead offering a call next week." Copilot writes a draft for you to review; it does not send it.
- "Pause the reactivation campaign for dormant 2025 leads." Copilot asks you to confirm first.
- "Create a sourcing agent for my approved Manchester agencies plan, 20 prospects a day." The agent is saved as a draft.
- "Start that agent." Copilot asks you to confirm, because a running agent spends money.

## Your workspace

- "What does ClientTurn know about my business?"
- "What should I set up first?"
- "Which connections need attention?"
- "What qualification questions do we ask?"
- "Raise a support ticket: bookings from Calendly are not appearing."

## Things Copilot will decline

Copilot will explain rather than act if you ask it to send a message, launch a campaign, suppress or erase a person, export their data, or delete an agent. See [Using Copilot](/help/copilot/using-copilot#what-copilot-will-never-do).

## Tips

- Open the relevant lead, prospect or campaign first, then ask about "this".
- Ask it to explain before it acts: "What would happen if I archived these?"
- Use the **Actions** tab to see everything it can do for your role.
