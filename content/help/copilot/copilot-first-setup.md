Setting up a new workspace starts with **Meet Copilot**, the first of six setup steps. It takes a minute and introduces the assistant you will use across the product.

## What the step shows

- **What Copilot does**: answers from your own data (every answer names where it came from), explains performance, drafts and prepares things for you to review, and takes routine actions you ask for.
- **The rules**, which are enforced by the product, not by the AI:
  - **It acts with your role.** Copilot has no permissions of its own.
  - **Lasting actions wait for you.** Sending, launching or starting an agent is described first and only runs when you confirm.
  - **Some things it can never do.** It cannot add anyone to the do-not-contact list, anonymise or erase a person, or export their data.

## Ask your first question

Pick a suggestion or write your own. The suggestions are read-only questions, so your first question can never change anything:

- "What should I set up first?"
- "What does ClientTurn know about my business?"
- "Which leads need attention?"
- "What can you do for me, and what will you always ask before doing?"

Once Copilot has answered, **Continue** becomes available.

## Skipping the step

Choose **Skip for now** to move on. Nothing else in setup depends on it. If Copilot cannot answer at that moment, you can skip and try later.

## Afterwards

Copilot is in the top bar on every page: choose **Copilot** to open it. See [Using Copilot](/help/copilot/using-copilot).
