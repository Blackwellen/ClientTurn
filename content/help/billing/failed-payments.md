If a charge fails — at the end of your trial or on a renewal — ClientTurn does not switch you off. Your data is never deleted because of a failed payment.

## The timeline

| When | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Days 0–2 after the first failed charge | **Everything.** Full access, so a card that expired over a weekend does not stop your follow-up |
| Day 3 onwards | **Sending and AI are paused.** New leads keep arriving and are stored. The inbox and every record stay readable. Queued messages are held, not dropped, and go out as soon as payment clears |
| Every day, for up to 30 days | The charge is **retried once a day**. Retrying stops the moment a payment succeeds |
| Day 25 | A final warning notification before cancellation |
| After the 30th failed retry | The subscription is cancelled and the workspace becomes **read-only**: data intact and exportable, no sending or AI |

## Fixing it

1. Open **Settings → Billing & Usage**.
2. Update your payment method.
3. Once a charge succeeds, full access returns straight away and retrying stops.

You get a billing notification when the first charge fails, when sending is paused, with the final warning, and when payment is received. Each links to where you can update your card.

## After cancellation

A cancelled workspace keeps its data. You can export records and re-subscribe at any time; re-subscribing restores the workspace. There is no second free trial.
