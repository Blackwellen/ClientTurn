If a subscription charge fails, at the end of your trial or on a renewal, ClientTurn does not switch you off straight away.

This page is about your **subscription**. If a one-off purchase fails (a top-up, an AI token pack or a voice minute pack), nothing else changes: the pack is simply not added, and your subscription, sending and voice carry on as normal.

## The timeline

| When | What you can do |
|---|---|
| Days 0–2 after the first failed charge | **Everything**, including voice calls. Full access, so a card that expired over a weekend does not stop your follow-up |
| Day 3 onwards | **Sending, AI and voice calls are paused.** New leads keep arriving and are stored. The inbox and every record stay readable. Queued messages are held, not dropped, and go out as soon as payment clears |
| Every day, for up to 30 days | The charge is **retried once a day**. Retrying stops the moment a payment succeeds |
| Day 25 | A final warning notification before cancellation |
| After the 30th failed retry | The subscription is cancelled and the workspace becomes **read-only**: data intact and exportable, no sending or AI |

## Fixing it

1. Open **Settings → Billing & Usage**.
2. Update your payment method.
3. Once a charge succeeds, full access returns straight away and retrying stops.

You get a billing notification when the first charge fails, when sending is paused, with the final warning, and when payment is received. Each links to where you can update your card.

## If your bank asks you to confirm a payment

Some cards need you to confirm a payment (3-D Secure) before it can be taken. When that happens you get a notification with a link to Stripe's secure page to confirm it. Nothing is paused while you do; if it stays unconfirmed, the timeline above applies.

## After cancellation

A cancelled workspace stays **read-only for 90 days**: you can see and export everything, and re-subscribing restores it exactly as it was. There is no second free trial. After 90 days the workspace and its lead data are deleted, as our privacy policy sets out. A dedicated phone number is released 14 days after the subscription ends; re-subscribe with voice before then to keep it. The dates are shown under **Settings → Billing & Usage → Subscription status**.
