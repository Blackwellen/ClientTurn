When a monthly allowance is not enough, you can buy more at any point in the month without changing plan. There are two kinds of top-up:

- **AI token packs** add to the assistant's AI allowance.
- **Message credits** add SMS segments or WhatsApp messages.

Only the workspace **owner** can buy them. Both are one-off card payments through Stripe, and neither sets up a recurring charge.

## AI token packs

| Pack | Tokens | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Small top-up | 500,000 | £15.00 |
| Medium top-up (best value) | 2,000,000 | £49.00 |
| Large top-up | 6,000,000 | £129.00 |

As a rough guide, 1M tokens is about 590 assistant replies. That figure deliberately over-estimates what one reply uses.

## SMS and WhatsApp credit bundles

| Bundle | Price | Per unit |
|---|---|---|
| 100 SMS segments | £9 | 9.0p |
| 500 SMS segments | £40 | 8.0p |
| 1,000 SMS segments | £75 | 7.5p |
| 250 WhatsApp messages | £15 | 6.0p |
| 1,000 WhatsApp messages | £50 | 5.0p |

One SMS credit is one UK SMS segment. A long text, or one containing an emoji, can use more than one segment. One WhatsApp credit is one message.

> **Important:** WhatsApp needs the Growth plan or above. WhatsApp credits bought on Starter cannot be used until you upgrade.

## How to buy

1. Open **Settings → Billing & Usage**.
2. For messages, go to **Message credits** and choose **Buy** next to a bundle. For AI, go to **AI allowance → Buy more tokens** and choose **Buy** next to a pack.
3. Pay in the Stripe checkout.
4. You come back to Billing & Usage. The credit is added once Stripe confirms the payment, usually within seconds.

When a limit is at 80% or more, the **Usage & limits** table also shows buttons such as **Add 500 for £40** next to that limit.

Stripe emails a receipt for each purchase. The **Purchase history** and **Recent top-ups** lists show each purchase and its status.

## The order credit is used in

For SMS and WhatsApp, each message is paid for in this order:

1. **Your plan's monthly allowance.**
2. **Top-up credit** you have bought.
3. **Overage**, but only if you switched **Allow automatic overage** on, only on a plan that has an overage price, and only while the month's overage stays under the cap you set.
4. Otherwise the message is **not sent**, and the reason is recorded on it.

A message is never split. Either all of its segments are covered or it does not go.

AI tokens work the same way for steps 1 and 2. There is **no AI overage**: when tokens run out, the assistant pauses until you top up or the next period starts. Your follow-up sequences and qualification rules keep running while it is paused. Only the AI wording and interpretation stop.

## Credit does not expire

- **Message credits** stay on your balance until they are used.
- **AI tokens you bought** carry over to the next period. Your plan's included tokens do not, because they are granted again each period. Included tokens are counted first, so what carries over is what is left of the tokens you bought.

## Refunds

If a top-up is refunded, the purchase shows as **Refunded**. Credit already added to your balance is not taken back automatically. Contact support if a balance needs correcting.
