**Settings → AI & selling** is where you tell ClientTurn how your business sells and sounds. Owners and admins can change it; everyone else can see the same settings read-only. None of these settings can override the hard rules: suppression and opt-outs, quiet hours, handover on a review decision, and the AI never quoting a price, a promise or availability it was not given.

## Start cautious, then widen

1. Set the automation level to **Suggest replies** first. For a week or two, read each draft in **Inbox** and send, edit or discard it.
2. Keep **Risk tolerance** on **Cautious** or **Balanced** while you learn how it behaves:
   - **Cautious** hands over whenever the assistant is not clearly confident it read the reply correctly.
   - **Balanced** hands over when confidence is low, or on a sensitive topic.
   - **Assertive** keeps the same safety floor as Balanced: low confidence and the hard rules still hand over.
3. Move to **Reply automatically** when the drafts you are discarding are rare. Discarded drafts are kept, so you can look back at what you rejected.

## Tell it how you sell

In **Sales behaviour**:

- Search for your industry, and set the **Business type**. This picks the scoring weights and qualification questions that suit your kind of business.
- Tick **How you sell**, the sales motions that apply to you.
- Choose a **Qualification depth**:
  - **Light** — ask only what unlocks the next step for your sales motion. Required questions are always asked.
  - **Standard** — ask the most useful questions and stop once enough is known.
  - **Thorough** — ask every configured question that applies.
- **Preferred methods** are internal planning heuristics for which question to ask next. They are never named to a buyer, and none is validated science; each shows the evidence behind it. See [Sales methods explained](/help/sales-knowledge/sales-methods-explained).

## Tell it how you sound

In **Brand**, fill in what makes your messages yours:

| Field | Good practice |
|---|---|
| **Tone of voice** | Describe it plainly, for example "plain-spoken and warm, never pushy" |
| **Value proposition** | One or two sentences on what you do for customers |
| **Key messages** | One per line; things worth saying when they are relevant |
| **Proof points** | One per line, and only claims you can stand behind. Never invent results |
| **Preferred call to action** | What a good next step looks like, for example "a 20-minute call" |
| **Claim restrictions** | One per line; things never to claim or promise |
| **Forbidden phrases** | One per line, 3 to 60 characters each. A draft that uses one is rejected and rewritten before anything is sent |
| **Good example messages** / **Bad example messages** | Up to five each. They are shown to the assistant as tone examples only, never as facts to repeat |

## Keep it inside budget

Set the **AI budget** limits so a busy month cannot surprise you, and use **Research depth: Light** if research is more than you need. See [AI budgets and model tiers](/help/ai-agents/ai-budgets-and-model-tiers).

## Keep the facts right

The assistant can only answer from what you have configured: your services, hours, service area, booking setup and business profile. Keep **Settings → Workspace** and **Settings → Business Profile** accurate, and publish price wording only if you are happy for it to be quoted.

## Record your lawful basis

If you contact people who have not given consent, record a legitimate interests assessment under **Compliance** on the same page: the purpose, why the processing is necessary, the balancing test, and when it will next be reviewed. See [Compliance overview](/help/compliance/compliance-overview).
