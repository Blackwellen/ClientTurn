Click a lead in **Leads** to open its drawer: the conversation, qualification result and next action, without leaving the list. For the full picture, choose **Open full page** in the drawer.

## The header

At the top of the page you will see:

- The lead's name, company and any badges: **Archived**, **Opted out**, or **With a person** when someone has taken the conversation over.
- **Tags** added automatically from what is known about the lead.
- **Owner**, **Deal** (which opens when the lead qualifies), **Score** and **Qualification**.
- The score's grade and dimensions, the evidence behind it, and what is **Worth finding out**.
- A contactability strip showing, for each channel (Email, SMS, WhatsApp, Social), whether this lead can be contacted and why. Beneath it, the WhatsApp opt-in on record, or **Record WhatsApp opt-in** to add one. See [Consent and WhatsApp opt-in](/help/compliance/consent-and-whatsapp-opt-in#recording-a-whatsapp-opt-in).

## The tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Conversation** | Every message on every channel, and the composer for replying |
| **Qualification** | The questions asked, the answers given and the result |
| **Score history** | Each score the lead has had, and what triggered it |
| **Attribution** | Every touch: the source, form, campaign, ad and UTM details for each time they enquired |
| **Activity & audit** | What happened to the lead and who did it |
| **AI** | What the conversation assistant did on this lead |
| **Data rights** | Suppress, restrict, export, anonymise or erase this person's data |

The tab is part of the page address, so you can link a colleague straight to it.

## Actions

**Message** takes you to the composer on the Conversation tab. It is greyed out, with the reason, when no channel may be used.

**Actions** opens a grouped menu:

- **Contact** — **Send booking link**; **Unsubscribe from everything…**
- **Pipeline** — **Add note…**; **Re-score**; **Take over** (automated follow-up stops and you reply yourself) or **Hand back** (the assistant resumes follow-up, and each message is re-checked before it sends)
- **Outcome** — **Mark won…** or **Mark lost…**, each asking for a reason (with optional **Reason type** chips such as Price, Timing or Competitor), which is recorded on the lead's deal. See [Opportunities, won and lost](/help/booking-and-sales/opportunities-and-won-lost)
- **Record** — **Archive…** (history is kept, and an owner or admin can **Restore** it)

Beside the menu you can change the **Owner** and move the **Deal stage**.

Options you cannot use are shown greyed out with the reason, for example because of your role or because the lead has opted out.
