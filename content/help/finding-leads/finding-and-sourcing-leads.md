ClientTurn finds new business in two ways.

- **Inbound**: people who come to you through an ad form, your website, a call or a referral become **leads** and can enter follow-up straight away. See [Lead sources explained](/help/finding-leads/lead-sources-explained).
- **Outbound**: companies you go looking for become **prospects** in **Find Leads**, and wait for review. See [Discovering companies with Find Leads](/help/finding-leads/find-leads-discovery).

## Start sourcing on a schedule

1. In **Find Leads**, build a search plan and start a sourcing run from it. Starting the run approves the plan.
2. Open **Agents → New agent** and choose **Sourcing agent**.
3. Under **Approved search plan**, choose that plan, and set the daily and monthly limits. The agent is saved as a draft.
4. Open it and choose **Start agent** when you are ready.

See [Setting up an agent](/help/ai-agents/setting-up-an-agent) for every option.

## Contact data is not permission

> **Important:** A discovered email address or phone number is contact data, not permission to send marketing.

Review provenance, recipient type and channel eligibility before contacting anyone. Suppression and opt-outs always apply, and sourced prospects stay in Find Leads until you decide they are appropriate to move to Leads.
