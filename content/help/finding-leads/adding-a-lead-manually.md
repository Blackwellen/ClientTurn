Use **Add lead** when someone contacts you outside your connected sources: a phone call, an email to your inbox, an event, a referral. The wizard records where the enquiry came from and what permission you have, then runs the same intake checks as every other source.

## Before you start

You need a member role or above. Adding a new service from inside the wizard needs an owner or admin.

## Add a lead

1. Open **Leads** and choose **Add lead**.
2. **Contact** — enter the person's name, company, and at least one of email, mobile or telephone. Postcode and address are optional. ClientTurn checks straight away for an existing lead or prospect with the same details and shows any match. If it is a different person, choose **Continue anyway**.
3. **Enquiry** — choose the **Service**, describe what they need under **Enquiry / interest**, and set the **Source** (Manual, Phone call, Walk-in, Referral, Event, Import, Pipedrive or Other) with any **Source detail**. **Estimated value** is optional and is an estimate, not revenue. Choose a **Conversion goal** such as a demo, a call or a quote. **Notes** are internal and never sent to the lead.
4. **Permission & contactability** — choose the **Relationship type** (how you know this person) and add **Consent evidence** where it matters: who referred them, what was said, and which contact methods are allowed. ClientTurn shows, per channel, whether this person can be contacted and why.
5. **Route & Start** — choose the **Assignee** and **Initial status**, optionally flag the lead for attention with a reason, and decide whether to switch on **Start follow-up for this lead**.
6. Choose **Create lead**. The lead opens in its drawer.

## If you found this person yourself

If you choose **We found this person or company** as the relationship, the wizard does not create a lead. Choose **Add to Find Leads instead** and the person is saved as a prospect for review, where cold-outreach rules apply before any message.

> **Important:** **Start follow-up for this lead** is only available when at least one channel is allowed for this person. If it is greyed out, the reason is shown beneath it, for example a suppressed number or a channel that is not connected.
