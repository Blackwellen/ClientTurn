This plan assumes you have finished [setting up your workspace](/help/getting-started/setting-up-your-workspace). Each step links to the detailed guide.

## Day 1: connect where your leads come from

- Connect your lead sources in **Settings → Connections**: [Meta Lead Ads](/help/finding-leads/meta-lead-ads), [Google Ads](/help/finding-leads/google-ads-lead-forms), [LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms](/help/finding-leads/linkedin-lead-gen-forms).
- If enquiries arrive through your website, send them in with the [API](/help/finding-leads/leads-from-the-api).
- Connect your calendar ([Google Calendar](/help/integrations/connecting-google-calendar) or [Calendly](/help/integrations/connecting-calendly)) so qualified leads can book.

## Day 2: bring in who you already know

- [Import a CSV](/help/finding-leads/importing-a-csv) of existing customers or past enquiries, choosing honestly how you know them. Leave **Start follow-up on imported leads** off until you have decided what to send.
- Connect your CRM, and decide whether to [import from it](/help/finding-leads/pulling-leads-from-your-crm).

## Day 3: set up email and your team

- [Connect your mailbox](/help/integrations/setting-up-email-outreach) and check **Sending domain health**.
- Invite colleagues in **Settings → Team**, giving each the lowest role they need.
- Connect [Slack](/help/integrations/connecting-slack) if your team lives there, and choose which alerts it gets.

## Day 4: tell ClientTurn how you sell

- Fill in **Settings → Business Profile** and **Settings → AI & selling**. See [AI guides and best practice](/help/ai-agents/ai-guides-best-practice).
- Turn the [conversation assistant](/help/ai-agents/the-conversation-assistant) on in **Suggest replies** mode, so you see every draft before it goes.

## Day 5: look for new business

- In **Find Leads**, describe your ideal customer and run a first, small search. See [Discovering companies with Find Leads](/help/finding-leads/find-leads-discovery).
- Review the prospects before approving anyone.

## Every day

- Check the **Dashboard** and **Inbox** for leads that need attention and conversations handed to a person. On the Dashboard, the **Booking-ready** card counts leads that are qualified but not yet booked; choose it to open that list under **Leads**.
- Work through anything left under **Finish setting up** at the top of the Dashboard.
- Ask Copilot "Which leads need attention?"

## End of the week

- Look at which sources produced qualified leads and bookings on the **Dashboard** and in **Analytics**.
- If the assistant's drafts needed little editing, consider moving it to **Reply automatically**.
- If a sourcing search worked well, turn it into a [sourcing agent](/help/ai-agents/setting-up-an-agent).
