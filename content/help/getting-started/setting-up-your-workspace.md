When you create a workspace, ClientTurn walks you through six steps before you reach the app. You can leave at any point with **Save & exit** and pick up where you left off; until setup is finished, signing in brings you back to it.

Each step has **Back**, **Save & exit** and **Continue** at the bottom. If **Continue** is not available, the reason is shown above it.

## 1. Meet Copilot

A short introduction to the assistant in your top bar: what it can do, the rules it follows, and a first question for it to answer from your workspace. Ask one question to continue, or choose **Skip for now**. See [Your first Copilot session](/help/copilot/copilot-first-setup).

## 2. Your Business

- **Business details**: **Business name** (required), **Industry**, **Website**, **Phone** and **Timezone**. Quiet hours and every timestamp use the timezone.
- **Opening hours**: when you are available to take calls and appointments.
- **Service area**: where you serve customers, if that matters to you.
- **Services**: at least one service you offer, with an optional average value. Choosing an industry offers one-tap suggestions.

## 3. Connect Leads

Connect your Meta account and choose the Facebook Page and lead forms to use, so new leads arrive automatically. This step is optional: you can go live and connect lead sources later in **Settings → Connections**. See [Lead sources explained](/help/finding-leads/lead-sources-explained).

## 4. Follow-Up

- Choose the channel for follow-up, and check your sender identity.
- Write the opening message sent to new leads, and the follow-up messages sent if they do not reply, with the delay before each.
- Set the quiet hours when nothing is sent, in your local time.
- Add opt-out wording. It must mention replying STOP.

## 5. Qualify & Book

- Write your qualifying questions and choose a rule for each answer: for example, whether an answer is required, or which answers disqualify. ClientTurn suggests a starting set.
- Choose the **Booking destination** for qualified leads: Calendly, Google Calendar, or a **Booking link** you paste in, or hand them to a person.

Every lead ends up **Qualified**, **Review** (a person decides) or **Not qualified**.

## 6. Test & Go Live

1. Check the readiness list. Business details, at least one service, qualification, a published follow-up sequence, messaging and booking (or handover) must all show **Ready**. A lead source and the test lead are recommended but optional.
2. Enter a name, phone number and service, and choose **Run test lead**. It follows the same path as a real lead, and you can watch it go from **Lead received** to **Test complete**. Test leads are left out of your analytics.
3. Choose **Go live**.

## After setup

You land on the **Dashboard**, and a short product tour starts. See [The product tour](/help/getting-started/the-product-tour) and [Your first week](/help/getting-started/first-week-guide).

Everything from setup can be changed later in **Settings**.
