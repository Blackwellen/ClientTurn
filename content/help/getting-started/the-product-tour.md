The first time you reach the **Dashboard** after setup, a short tour starts on its own. It points out where everything lives, one highlighted area at a time.

## Moving through it

- Use **Next** and **Back** on each caption, or the arrow keys. The last step has **Finish**.
- Choose **Skip tour**, the close button or **Esc** to leave at any time.
- Where a step belongs to another page, the tour takes you there.

Once you finish or leave the tour, it does not start on its own again.

## What it covers

1. **Dashboard** — your day at a glance: new leads, what needs attention, bookings and how each source is performing.
2. **Leads** — every enquiry and its whole history in one list.
3. **Follow-Up** — the follow-up sequence, qualification questions and rules, quiet hours and booking behaviour.
4. **Reactivation** — re-engaging older enquiries that went quiet.
5. **Settings** — your business profile, team, messaging and connections.
6. **Revenue control** — the Dashboard cards that show where value is won or lost.
7. **Your leads** — filtering, sorting and quick filters.
8. **The lead drawer** — the conversation, qualification result and next action, without leaving the list.
9. **Inbox** — replies from every channel, and anything handed to a person.
10. **Copilot** — asking about your workspace in plain English.
11. **Help** — searching the help centre, raising a ticket and checking system status.
12. **Connect your tools** — lead sources, mailbox, calendar and CRM in **Settings → Connections**.

## Replay the tour

Choose **Take the product tour** in either place:

- the round help button at the bottom right of every page, on its **Help** tab;
- the **Help** page, at the top.
