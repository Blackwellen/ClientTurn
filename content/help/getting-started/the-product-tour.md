ClientTurn has two kinds of tour. Both highlight one part of the page at a time, with a caption beside it and a lime line from the caption to the part it describes.

- **The first-use tour** starts on its own the first time you reach the **Dashboard** after setup. It shows where everything lives.
- **Page tours** are three to six steps on a single page: what each part is for and what to do there. Each one starts on its own the first time you open that page, but only after the first-use tour is finished or skipped. Only one tour ever runs at a time.

## Moving through a tour

- Use **Next** and **Back** on each caption, or the arrow keys. The last step has **Finish**.
- Choose **Skip tour**, the close button or **Esc** to leave at any time.
- Where a step belongs to another page, the tour takes you there.
- On a phone the caption is a sheet at the bottom or top of the screen, with the line running to the part it describes.
- Steps about something your plan or role does not include are left out.

Once you finish or leave a tour, it does not start on its own again. Each page's tour is remembered separately.

## What the first-use tour covers

1. **Your workspace**: where you are.
2. **Dashboard**: your day at a glance.
3. **Agents**: background workers that run within the limits you set.
4. **Leads**: every enquiry and its whole history in one list.
5. **Find Leads**, if your plan includes it: searching for businesses that match your ideal customer.
6. **Follow-Up**: the follow-up sequence, qualification questions and rules, quiet hours and booking behaviour.
7. **Reactivation**: re-engaging older enquiries that went quiet.
8. **Analytics**, if your plan includes it: trends across the whole journey.
9. **Settings**: your business profile, team, messaging and connections.
10. **Revenue control**: the Dashboard cards that show where value is won or lost.
11. **Your leads** and **the lead drawer**: filtering, and the conversation, qualification result and next action without leaving the list.
12. **Inbox**: replies from every channel, and anything handed to a person.
13. **Copilot**: asking about your workspace in plain English.
14. **Help**: searching the help centre, raising a ticket and checking system status.
15. **Connect your tools**: lead sources, mailbox, calendar and CRM in **Settings → Connections**.

## Page tours

There is a page tour for the **Dashboard**, **Leads**, **Follow-Up**, **Reactivation**, **Settings**, **Find Leads**, **Agents**, **Analytics** and **Inbox**. For example, the Leads tour covers **Add lead** and **Import**, the quick filters, search and **Filters**, and opening a lead. The Follow-Up tour ends on the **Qualification** view.

## Replay a tour

- **Tour this page**: the compass button in the top bar starts the current page's tour. On smaller screens it shows the compass only.
- **Take the product tour** replays the first-use tour, from the round help button at the bottom right of every page (on its **Help** tab) or from the top of the **Help** page.
- **Tour a page** on the **Help** page lists every page tour. Choosing one takes you to that page and starts it.
