ClientTurn follows up every enquiry the moment it arrives, qualifies it against your rules, and books the ones worth your time, while keeping a record of where each came from.

## Where things live

The sidebar takes you to each area:

| Area | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Lead outcomes, bookings, revenue control and anything needing attention |
| **Agents** | Background agents that source, chase bookings and re-engage old leads |
| **Inbox** | Replies from every channel, suggested replies and handovers |
| **Leads** | Every enquiry, its conversation, qualification and history |
| **Find Leads** | Prospects you are researching, kept separate until you review them (not on every plan) |
| **Follow-Up** | Your follow-up sequence and qualification questions |
| **Reactivation** | Campaigns that re-engage older enquiries |
| **Analytics** | Performance by source, campaign and funnel stage (not on every plan) |
| **Settings** | Workspace, Connections, Business Profile, AI & selling, Team, Developer, Data Controls, and Billing & Usage |

**Copilot** is in the top bar on every page, and the round help button at the bottom right opens help, your support tickets and system status.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and deleting the workspace |
| **Admin** | Everything except deleting the workspace |
| **Member** | Work leads and messages; cannot change workspace settings |
| **Viewer** | Read leads and reporting |

To invite colleagues, open **Settings → Team**. Owners and admins can invite people and choose their role.
