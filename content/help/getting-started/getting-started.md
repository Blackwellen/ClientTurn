ClientTurn follows up every enquiry the moment it arrives, qualifies it against your rules, and books the ones worth your time, while keeping a record of where each came from.

## Creating your account

Anyone can sign up. There is no invitation needed.

1. On the sign-up page, enter your **First name**, **Last name**, **Work email** and a **Password**, tick the box to accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and choose **Create your account**.
2. Open the confirmation email and follow its link.
3. You arrive at **Start your 14-day free trial**. Choose a plan and add a card. See [Your free trial](/help/billing/free-trial).
4. Setup starts. Its first step is where you name your workspace: sign-up does not ask for a business name. See [Setting up your workspace](/help/getting-started/setting-up-your-workspace).

You can use **Sign up with Google** instead (on the sign-in page it reads **Continue with Google**). If there is no ClientTurn account for your Google address, one is created and you go straight to trial checkout. If you chose a plan on the pricing page, it is still highlighted when you get there. If you were invited to a workspace, Google signs you into that workspace instead.

## Where things live

The sidebar takes you to each area:

| Area | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Lead outcomes, bookings, revenue control and anything needing attention |
| **Agents** | Background agents that source, chase bookings and re-engage old leads |
| **Inbox** | Replies from every channel, suggested replies and handovers |
| **Leads** | Every enquiry, its conversation, qualification and history |
| **Find Leads** | Prospects you are researching, kept separate until you review them (not on every plan) |
| **Follow-Up** | Your follow-up sequence and qualification questions |
| **Reactivation** | Campaigns that re-engage older enquiries |
| **Analytics** | Performance by source, campaign and funnel stage (not on every plan) |
| **Settings** | Workspace, Connections, Business Profile, AI & selling, Team, Developer, Data Controls, and Billing & Usage |

**Copilot** is in the top bar on every page, and the round help button at the bottom right opens help, your support tickets and system status.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and deleting the workspace |
| **Admin** | Settings and managing members and viewers. No billing, workspace export or deletion, and cannot manage other admins |
| **Member** | Work leads and messages; cannot change workspace settings |
| **Viewer** | Read leads and reporting |

To invite colleagues, open **Settings → Team**. Owners and admins can invite members and viewers; only the owner can invite an admin or make someone an admin. See [Team settings](/help/settings/team-settings).
