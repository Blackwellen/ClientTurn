Look at **Settings → System check → Booking**.

## Common reasons

| What System check says | What it means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Calendar: Needs attention | Booking is set to Google Calendar or Calendly, but it isn't connected (or needs reconnecting) | **Settings → Connections**: connect or reconnect it |
| Booking link: Off by choice | No calendar and no booking link, so qualified leads are handed to you to book by hand | Connect a calendar, or add a booking link in **Settings → Workspace → Booking** |
| AI may book meetings: Off by choice | **Book meetings** is off in **What the AI may do**, so the assistant never offers times, or the AI assistant is off, so only your fixed follow-up steps send the booking link | **Settings → AI & selling → What the AI may do**, or **Settings → Workspace → AI assistant** |
| AI may book meetings: Needs attention | The assistant may book but there's no working calendar or link to book into | Connect a calendar or add a booking link |

A lead is only offered a booking once they qualify under your rules. If one lead wasn't offered times, check its **Qualification** tab.
