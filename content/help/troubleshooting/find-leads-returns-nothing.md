Look at **Settings → System check → Find Leads**.

## Common reasons

| What System check says | What it means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Find Leads on your plan: Off by choice | Finding new prospects isn't on your plan | **Settings → Billing & Usage** |
| Search runs: Needs attention | Every search run for this period is used | Wait for the period to reset, or upgrade in **Settings → Billing & Usage** |
| Verified prospects: Needs attention | Every verified prospect for this period is used | As above |
| Company search sources: Needs attention | No company search source is available on ClientTurn's side right now | Nothing to change in your settings; contact support |

## If everything is ready

A search that finds nobody is usually too narrow. Try:

1. A wider location, or several nearby towns.
2. Fewer required signals.
3. A lower minimum grade.

ClientTurn only finds contacts from free, first-party sources, such as a company's own website and Companies House. It never buys contact data, so a company that publishes no team or contact page may be found without a named contact.
