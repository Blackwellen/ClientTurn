When the conversation assistant passes a lead to a person, it also prepares a **brief**: what you need to know to take over without reading every message.

## When the assistant hands over

The assistant hands a conversation to your team when, for example:

| Reason | Priority |
|---|---|
| The lead complained, or it is an emergency | Urgent |
| The lead asked for a person | High |
| The lead is ready to buy, or the deal is above your value limit | High |
| A calendar or other connection failed | High |
| A reply could not be matched to your qualification rules | Normal |
| The assistant was not confident it understood the reply | Normal |
| The lead asked about pricing you have not published | Normal |
| The question is outside what the assistant is set up to handle | Normal |
| There is no next question to ask | Normal |
| The AI budget for this lead is used up | Normal |

You are told in the app, and in Slack if you have Slack alerts on. See [Workspace settings](/help/settings/workspace-settings).

## What is in the brief

The **Lead brief** is built only from what ClientTurn has recorded. Nothing in it is written by AI:

- the lead's score and grade, and why,
- their tags,
- the answers they gave, with any inferred answers marked **(inferred)**,
- objections they raised,
- anything your business has already promised them,
- what is still unknown,
- the meeting, if one is booked or requested,
- a recommended approach for your sales motion.

Above it is a **30-second brief**: a few sentences summarising the lead. AI writes it from the recorded brief only, never from the raw conversation. It is then checked: if it mentions any number, date or name that is not in the recorded brief, it is thrown away and a plain summary built from the facts is used instead. You can trust that nothing in it was invented.

## Where to find it

Open the conversation in the **Inbox**. The assistant panel shows the handover, its priority, the 30-second brief and the **Lead brief**.

If you have a connected CRM that accepts notes, the brief is also added to the contact there.

## Dealing with a handover

In the assistant panel:

- **I'll handle this** claims the handover for you.
- **Resolve** marks it dealt with, with an optional note of **What was done?**. Choose **Resolve and keep it with me** to keep replying yourself, or **Resolve and hand back** to return the conversation to the assistant.
- **Not needed** withdraws a handover that should not have been raised.

The assistant never takes a conversation back by itself. Only a person can hand it back. See [Taking over a conversation](/help/booking-and-sales/taking-over-a-conversation).
