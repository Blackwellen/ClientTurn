ClientTurn never tells a lead a meeting is booked unless something has confirmed it. If you do not use a connected calendar, or the calendar cannot confirm a time, **you** are the confirmation.

## Choose manual booking

1. Open **Settings → Workspace**.
2. Under **How qualified leads book**, choose **Manual link or handover**.
3. Optionally, enter a **Booking link**, for example your own booking page. It must start with `https://`.
4. Choose **Save booking settings**.

## What a qualified lead gets

- **With a booking link:** the lead is sent the link to book a time themselves.
- **Without one:** the lead is told someone will be in touch, the conversation is handed to your team, and you are notified that a qualified lead is ready to book.

## When a lead asks for a specific time

If the conversation assistant is talking to a lead who names a time, and no calendar can confirm it:

1. The time is recorded as a **request**, not a booking.
2. The lead is told the time has been requested and is not confirmed yet, and that someone from the team will be in touch to confirm it.
3. The request holds the time, so it is not offered to anyone else.
4. It appears on your **Dashboard** under **Awaiting confirmation**.

The same happens with Google Calendar if the connection has stopped working.

## Confirm or decline a request

On the **Dashboard**, in **Awaiting confirmation**, each request has two buttons:

- **Confirm time:** the booking becomes **Scheduled**, the lead moves to **Booked**, automatic follow-up stops, and booking reminders start if you have them on.
- **Decline:** the request is cancelled.

Confirming is the only way a request becomes a booking. Let the lead know the outcome yourself; ClientTurn does not send a message when you confirm or decline.

## Record what happened

After a scheduled meeting, record the outcome from **Upcoming bookings** on the Dashboard:

- **It went ahead**
- **They did not turn up**
- **It was cancelled**

A no-show or cancellation flags the lead for your team's attention, so it does not quietly disappear. Changing a booking here does not change it in Calendly or Google Calendar.

Members, admins and owners can confirm, decline and record outcomes.
