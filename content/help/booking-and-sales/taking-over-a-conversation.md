You can take any conversation away from the assistant and automated follow-up at any time and reply yourself. Nothing is sent automatically while you have it, and only a person can hand it back.

## Take over

Choose either:

- on the lead page, **Actions → Take over**, or
- in the **Inbox**, **Take over** in the assistant panel.

Automated follow-up for that lead stops straight away, and the assistant will not reply. There is no moment when both you and the assistant could reply.

Members, admins and owners can take over.

ClientTurn also takes over for you, handing the conversation to your team, when the assistant hands over. That is a last resort, for example when the lead asks for a person or complains. When the assistant only needs one thing confirmed, it asks your team in the background and keeps the conversation, so nothing is taken over. See [The handoff brief](/help/booking-and-sales/handoff-brief).

## While you have the conversation

- Reply from the lead page (**Message**) or the **Inbox**.
- When the lead replies, you get a notification: "New reply on a conversation you are handling".
- The lead shows the **Needs human** tag.
- Your replies still go through the normal checks: opt-outs and suppression always apply. Daily sending limits do not hold back replies you type yourself.

If the assistant is in **Suggest replies** mode, you may also see a **Suggested reply** in the Inbox. You can **Edit**, **Send** or **Discard** it. A suggested reply is never sent without a person approving it.

## Hand back

When you are done, choose **Actions → Hand back** on the lead page, or **Hand back** in the Inbox. The assistant resumes follow-up, and each message is checked again before it is sent.

Hand back is not available when:

- the lead opted out,
- the lead is archived (restore it first),
- the lead is won or lost,
- the lead is booked. Follow-up has done its job, though booking reminders still send.

The assistant never takes a conversation back on a timer. A quiet conversation is not the same as a finished one.
