Sometimes a lead asks for the checkout link and then gets distracted. The assistant can follow up for you, gently and within your rules.

## Turn it on or change it

Open **Settings → Business Profile → Selling: direct close**, then **Follow up abandoned checkouts**. It is on by default once direct close is on.

- **First reminder after (hours):** 24 by default.
- **Reminders at most:** 2 by default. Choose 0 to send none.
- **Hours between reminders:** 48 by default, so the defaults send at 24 hours and 72 hours.

## What the assistant sends

Each reminder is written by the assistant for that lead, with the same checkout link. It answers the likely hesitation in a sentence and invites a reply. It is checked like every other message the assistant writes:

- the only price it may state is the price text on that link;
- no discount beyond your maximum;
- no pressure or false urgency;
- it never says the lead has paid.

A reminder that cannot pass these checks is simply not sent.

## When reminders stop

Reminders stop, and are never sent, when:

- the lead pays (or a payment for them is waiting for you to confirm);
- the lead opts out or is suppressed;
- a colleague takes the conversation over, or the lead is paused, archived, won or lost;
- you turn the setting off.

Every reminder is also checked again at the moment it would send, including quiet hours.

## Which channel

A lead who has been talking to the assistant hears on the same channel. A lead who has not replied gets the reminder by email where possible, and by SMS only when it fits your SMS budget.

A week after the last reminder, an unpaid link is marked **Expired** on the lead page. If the lead pays later, the payment still counts.
