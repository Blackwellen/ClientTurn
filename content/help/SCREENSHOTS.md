(fictional `.example` B2B data, no provider connections, every queued job parked so nothing could
send), then deleted. Files follow the README section 5 contract:
`public/help/screenshots/<category>/<slug>-<n>.png`, annotated with numbered markers and the
branded caption band, and listed in each article's `screenshots:` frontmatter. The Status column
says what was captured or why a row was skipped. Rows skipped because they need a live provider
connection, a real Stripe object, a real send or an AI call need a sandbox with test credentials.
